Through **Brick City Glam**, Artify Ocala has provided opportunities to numerous performers. This project has supported and encouraged those exploring the performing arts through programs such as the Dazzling Daycamp Workshop series and Performer Development Workshops.

## Upcoming performances

For the most up-to-date events and happenings, visit our community partners — links are available from the main site navigation and home page.

## Collaborations

We host our signature series, **The Menagerie at Mutiny**, bringing themed burlesque cabaret shows to Ocala, and we partner with several organizations to bring performance arts to events throughout the community.

We provide entertainment for attendees at the **Fine Arts For Ocala** annual festival. We work with individual local artists to enhance exhibit openings with live acts and interpretive dance. Our work with local galleries such as **Magnolia Art Xchange**, **NOMA**, and the **Brick City Center for the Arts** helps patrons experience a more fully immersive art experience.

## Join our team

If you are interested in working with us as a performer or production crew member, we would love to hear from you. We are always expanding the scope of our performance outreach and growing new opportunities for individuals to express their creative selves. No prior experience is necessary — just a desire to be part of the fun.

Complete the **casting** form linked from the home page to get started.
