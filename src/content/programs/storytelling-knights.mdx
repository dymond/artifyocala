**Storytelling Knights** is a project at the forefront of a growing cultural resurgence — a rekindling of shared experiences in tabletop roleplaying games (TTRPGs). With a blend of fantasy and reality, our mission is to bring people together for moments that are thrilling and deeply connective.

Our events have seen incredible transformations and unforgettable stories: skeleton breakdancers, ethereal light beings, vampires, failed trapeze artists, chicken dragons, and more. These shared moments are magical — laughter, dice rolls, and collaborative storytelling at the table are what we celebrate.

## Heroes wanted

There is a profound need for physical connection and non-digital play. Storytelling Knights offers a genuine, analog communal experience where friends gather, share snacks, and go on adventures together.

We celebrate the diversity of TTRPGs. You can become anyone or anything — science fiction, fantasy, horror, or real-life settings. Whether you want to embody a feline creature, a fearsome orc, or a shape-shifting entity on a cosmic voyage, the possibilities are broad. Our goal is to make the hobby accessible and enjoyable.

Rules can feel daunting when you have only seen seasoned players on streaming shows. We believe in starting simple: gather with friends, craft a narrative, roll the dice, and let the story unfold. We work to lower barriers and emphasize **fun**.

Our events are built for **inclusivity**. Newcomers receive straightforward character concepts and freedom to imagine who they want to be. The focus is a vibrant shared narrative, togetherness, and joy.
